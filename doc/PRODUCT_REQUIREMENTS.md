# Usef Farahmand Digital Experience

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Product Summary

Usef Farahmand Digital Experience is the next generation of my personal website.

It is not a traditional portfolio or resume.

It is an interactive digital experience designed to communicate who I am, what I build, how I think, and why I build software.

The website should represent both my personal brand and Jolly Panda Studio while remaining focused on the products rather than myself.

---

# Vision

Create a memorable personal website that combines excellent UX, purposeful motion, modern engineering, and storytelling.

Visitors should leave understanding not only what I have built, but also how I approach problems and why I enjoy building digital products.

---

# Problem Statement

Most personal portfolio websites:

- look almost identical
- focus only on achievements
- feel static
- provide little insight into the creator's thinking
- become outdated quickly

The new website should solve these problems by presenting an evolving digital experience instead of a static portfolio.

# Accessibility Requirements

Support:

- Reduced Motion
- Proper Contrast
- Semantic HTML
- ARIA Labels

Target:

WCAG AA Compliance

---

# SEO Requirements

The website must include:

- Semantic HTML
- Structured Data
- Open Graph
- Twitter Cards
- Dynamic Metadata
- XML Sitemap
- robots.txt
- Canonical URLs

---

# Motion Requirements

Motion should:

- Support usability
- Guide attention
- Improve clarity
- Provide interaction feedback

Motion must never distract from content.

Anime.js will be used for purposeful micro-interactions and transitions.

---

# Content Strategy

Content should:

- Teach
- Document
- Explain
- Inspire curiosity
- Share lessons learned

Avoid generic motivational content.

---

# Future Requirements

Future versions will introduce:

- Interactive World
- Custom CMS
- Admin Dashboard
- Analytics Dashboard
- Media Library
- Timeline Management
- Project Management
- Article Management

The architecture must be prepared for these additions without requiring major restructuring.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The MVP is considered successful when:

- Visitors understand my identity within a few seconds.
- Navigation feels intuitive.
- Projects are the primary focus.
- Storytelling is stronger than self-promotion.
- Motion improves the experience.
- The website performs exceptionally.
- The design reflects craftsmanship and attention to detail.
- Future expansion can be achieved without architectural changes.
